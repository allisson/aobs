aobs boot-checklist run record
==============================

The procedure is docs/boot-checklist.md; its section "The run record" is the format, and
docs/release.md steps 4 and 9 say when this file is written and when it is closed.

Items 1 through 26 are answered at step 4, on the ISO the release was built from, before the tag is
pushed. Items 27 and 28 cannot have answers before the release exists; they are below the
post-publication rule, appended after step 9. They are deferred, not forgotten.

Verdicts are pass, fail or deviated. A deviated item carries prose saying what was done instead. A
record containing a fail does not get published.


Release
-------

release:        v0.0.0
git-commit:
iso-sha256:
run-started:                       # UTC date, from the operator's own machine, not the appliance
run-finished:
operator:


Machine
-------

Transcribed by hand from outside the appliance. No serial numbers, no MAC addresses, no disk
identifiers: this file is published, and a resemblance aid does not need to identify the hardware to
a third party.

make-model:
cpu:
ram:
firmware:                          # UEFI or BIOS, and its version
graphics:                          # adapter, and the framebuffer geometry it gave
camera:                            # make and model, and the pixel formats it offered
keyboard:                          # USB, PS/2 or built-in

or a specific device (items 7, 10, 12, 16, 19, 22 and 23 as the list stands). Items 9 and 14 carry a
mandatory `observed:` instead: a bare pass on them says nothing.

--- post-publication ---

Everything above this rule is the published asset, byte for byte, and is covered by the manifest's
signature. Everything below it exists only in the committed copy at docs/boot-runs/<release>.txt and
is covered by git history alone. Nothing above the rule is ever edited; a correction is a new line
here, naming the item it corrects.

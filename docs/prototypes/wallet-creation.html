What this is asking

Between “create a new wallet” and “the mnemonic is confirmed recorded” sit six decisions the map never made: where the optional dice sit, what the screen says while the CSPRNG is still gathering, how 24 words are laid out for someone copying them onto paper, what counts as proof they were written down, what else may share the screen with a seed, and what a wrong confirmation destroys. Walk the flow and each one stops being an argument. Every contested answer is a switch on the right — flip one and walk it again.

The phrase is generated from crypto.getRandomValues, mixed with your dice exactly as #8 specifies, and checksummed with a real SHA-256 — so the words are a phrase you could genuinely restore. Don’t put money on it.

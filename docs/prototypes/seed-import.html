Prototype — issue #18 — seed importtype into it: the wordlist and the checksum are real

What this is asking

A user has a recovery phrase written on paper and a physical keyboard. Between those two facts sit seven decisions: how a word is entered, what happens to a word that is not on the list, when the checksum is allowed to speak, whether the final word is offered as a choice, how the length is declared, how a mistake is corrected, and what a rejection destroys. Type a phrase in and each one stops being an argument. Every contested answer is a switch on the right — flip one and retype.

Try: absurd avoid scissors anxiety gather lottery category door army half long camera — a real, valid 12-word phrase.
